About Sealubbers

A pirate RPG built as an independent long-term project.

Sealubbers is an open-world pirate RPG set in Krucia, an ocean world built around treasure hunting, crew building, ship combat, exploration, and the idea of leaving your own story written into the stars.

The game and site are being developed independently under Plunder LLC. This site exists to explain the world, show active progress, and give people one place to follow along as the project grows.

What This Project Is Trying To Do

The goal is to make a pirate adventure that feels expansive without becoming impersonal. That means the world needs memorable places, factions with their own identity, crew members who matter, and systems that connect back to the fantasy of becoming a captain rather than just clearing objectives.

Sealubbers is meant to reward curiosity. Sailing to a strange island, recruiting someone unexpected, tracking down treasure clues, or choosing how to handle a dangerous ship should all feel like part of the same larger journey.

The Game

An open-world pirate RPG focused on exploration, crew progression, quests, treasure hunting, and naval combat.

The Site

A home for development updates, world details, system breakdowns, and the public-facing information around the project.

The Approach

Independent development with an emphasis on making the game feel distinctive rather than rushing it out before the core ideas feel right.

Who Is Behind It

Sealubbers is being developed independently by Captain Rubin through Plunder LLC. This is not a large studio project with a separate marketing site and publishing arm behind it. The development work, updates, and public communication all come from the same small operation.

That is also why this site is intentionally direct. It is here to show what the game is, what progress is being made, and where people can keep up with it, without turning the project into something that feels overproduced or impersonal.

How To Follow Along

The best place to see real progress is the development section, where larger updates are posted when there is something meaningful to show. The support page also links out to the main social channels for people who want shorter updates, clips, or community activity between bigger posts.
